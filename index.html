About the app

Tenet is an Android app for college students and working professionals (18+) who want to scroll less. It measures your own screen time, then builds a plan that lowers your daily limit for social, short-video and game apps a little at a time, toward a two-hour daily target.

  • Daily limits for social, video and game apps, with a calm block screen when a limit runs out.
  • YouTube stays open; only time in the Shorts player counts.
  • A Reels and Shorts counter, streaks, goals and weekly stats.
  • Focus sessions: calls, text messages (SMS, not chat apps), Google Maps, emergency apps and Android Settings always stay open, and you can end a session early at any time.
  • An optional friends leaderboard and an optional AI coach.

Tenet is free and supported by optional rewarded ads. It is a self-control tool, not a medical or therapeutic service. Not affiliated with or endorsed by YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, Snapchat, TikTok, Moj or Josh.

Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This policy explains how Divya Pathak ("we", "us"), the developer of the Android app "Tenet: Scroll With Intent" ("Tenet"), handles personal data. We decide how this data is used (we are the data controller, and in India the "Data Fiduciary"). Contact: dev.divya016@gmail.com.

1. Data that stays on your phone

  • Screen time. With your permission (Android "Usage access"), Tenet reads how long you use each app, to build your plan, show your stats and check your limits. Per-app usage and app names stay on your phone and are never sent to us or anyone else. Only totals leave the phone: one combined weekly number if you join the friends leaderboard, and a short progress summary (minutes, limits, streak) if you use the live coach (section 2).
  • Which app is open (the Tenet guard). With your permission (Android "Accessibility"), the guard sees the name of the app on screen so it can block apps over today's limit and keep focus sessions locked. In YouTube, it checks whether the Shorts player is showing. In YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and Snapchat, only while their Reels or Shorts player is on screen, it reads the creator name and caption of the clip to count each clip once; that text is turned into a code in memory straight away and is never stored or sent. In TikTok, Moj and Josh it counts full-screen swipes and reads no text. It never reads your passwords, what you type, or text in any other app. To leave a blocked app or the Shorts player, it may press Home or Back for you. It never blocks Android Settings.
  • Your plan, goals, focus sessions, streaks, notes you write, pause choices, settings and the guard history are stored on your phone.

Uninstalling Tenet or clearing its storage deletes this data.

3. Ads and consent

Tenet shows rewarded ads only when you choose to watch one: to add 10 minutes to a limit (up to 3 times per category a day), or to end a focus session early. You can always end a focus session without ads after a short wait. In the EEA, UK and other places where it is required, Google's consent form asks for your choice, and you can change it in the app under Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy choices. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings.

Supabase, Inc. (hosting, database and sign-in), Groq, Inc. (AI replies, USA) and Google (sign-in and ads) process data on our behalf or under their own terms. No method is perfectly secure.
