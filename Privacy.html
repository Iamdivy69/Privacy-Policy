About the app

Tenet is an Android app for college students and working professionals (18+) who want to scroll less. It measures your own screen time, then builds a plan that lowers your daily limit for social, short-video and game apps a little at a time, toward a two-hour daily target.

  • Daily limits for social, video and game apps, with a calm block screen when a limit runs out.
  • YouTube stays open; only time in the Shorts player counts.
  • A Reels and Shorts counter, streaks, goals and weekly stats.
  • Focus sessions: calls, messages, maps, emergency apps and Android Settings always stay open, and you can end a session early at any time.
  • An optional friends leaderboard and an optional AI coach.

Tenet is free and supported by optional rewarded ads. It is a self-control tool, not a medical or therapeutic service. Not affiliated with or endorsed by YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, Snapchat, TikTok, Moj or Josh.

Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

This policy explains how Divya Pathak ("we", "us"), the developer of the Android app "Tenet: Scroll With Intent" ("Tenet"), handles personal data. We decide how this data is used (we are the data controller, and in India the "Data Fiduciary"). Contact: dev.divya016@gmail.com.

1. Data that stays on your phone

  • Screen time. With your permission (Android "Usage access"), Tenet reads how long you use each app, to build your plan, show your stats and check your limits. Per-app usage and app names stay on your phone and are never sent to us or anyone else. Only totals per category (for example, minutes in social apps) leave the phone, and only if you join the friends leaderboard or use the live coach (section 2).
  • Which app is open (the Tenet guard). With your permission (Android "Accessibility"), the guard sees the name of the app on screen so it can block apps over today's limit and keep focus sessions locked. In YouTube, it checks whether the Shorts player is showing. In YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and Snapchat, only while their Reels or Shorts player is on screen, it reads the creator name and caption of the clip to count each clip once; that text is turned into a code in memory straight away and is never stored or sent. In TikTok, Moj and Josh it counts full-screen swipes and reads no text. It never reads your passwords, what you type, or text in any other app. To leave a blocked app or the Shorts player, it may press Home or Back for you. It never blocks Android Settings.
  • Your plan, goals, focus sessions, streaks, notes you write, pause choices, settings and the guard history are stored on your phone.

Uninstalling Tenet or clearing its storage deletes this data.

3. Ads and consent

Tenet shows rewarded ads only when you choose to watch one: to add 10 minutes to a limit (up to 3 times per category a day), or to end a focus session early. You can always end a focus session without ads after a short wait. In the EEA, UK and other places where it is required, Google's consent form asks for your choice, and you can change it in the app under Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy choices. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings.

4. Legal bases (EEA and UK)

  • Contract: accounts, backup, leaderboard and coach, which you ask us to provide.
  • Consent: the live coach, the leaderboard's reel count, and personalised ads where consent is required. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legitimate interests: security, fraud prevention and handling reports.

5. Service providers and transfers

Supabase, Inc. (hosting, database and sign-in), Groq, Inc. (AI replies, USA) and Google (sign-in, ads and fonts) process data on our behalf or under their own terms. Some of them process data outside your country, including in the USA; where the law requires it, these transfers rely on the providers' standard safeguards, such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

6. How long we keep data

  • Data on your phone: until you uninstall Tenet or clear its storage.
  • Account, backup and leaderboard data: until you delete your account. Leaving the leaderboard deletes your name, numbers, friendships and group memberships at once.
  • Coach reports: until you delete your account.
  • Coach messages: not stored by us after the reply.

7. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, export or delete your data, withdraw consent, or object to processing, by emailing dev.divya016@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days. In the app you can delete your account, leave the leaderboard, turn backup off, stop using the live coach and change ad choices. You can also complain to your data protection authority (EEA), the ICO (UK) or, after contacting us first, the Data Protection Board of India.

8. Security

Data sent from the app is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Data on our server is protected by per-account access rules, so each account can reach only its own data. No method is perfectly secure.

9. Adults only

Tenet is intended for people aged 18 and older and is not directed at children. The app asks you to confirm you are 18 or older before setup. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If we learn that we have, we delete the account and its data. Contact us if you believe this has happened.

10. Changes

We will update this page and the date above when this policy changes, and tell you in the app about significant changes.

Delete your Tenet account

In the app: open Tenet → Profile → Delete account. Your account and its cloud backup are deleted at once.

Without the app: email dev.divya016@gmail.com from the email address of your account, with the subject "Delete my Tenet account". We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted: your account (name, email, sign-in details), cloud backup, leaderboard profile, friendships and group memberships, and coach reports. Nothing is kept afterwards. Data stored only on your phone is removed when you uninstall the app.
